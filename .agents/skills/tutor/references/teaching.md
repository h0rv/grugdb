# Teaching references

- [Organizing instruction and study to improve student learning](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/1),
  Institute of Education Sciences. Recommendation 7 supports questions that ask
  learners to explain their reasoning. Recommendation 2 supports combining
  worked examples with practice. Explain concepts directly and choose examples
  that leave the active exercise for the learner to solve.
- [Socratic teaching scaffolds](https://github.com/lyndonkl/claude/blob/main/skills/socratic-teaching-scaffolds/SKILL.md).
  Use predictions and concrete counterexamples to examine a misconception.
  Reduce hints as the learner becomes independent. Avoid a fixed sequence of
  questions.
- [Tutor skill](https://github.com/bevibing/tutor-skills/blob/main/skills/tutor/SKILL.md).
  Focus on specific misconceptions. Ask permission before recording progress. Do
  not infer mastery from a few answers.
- [Agent Skills specification](https://agentskills.io/specification). Defines
  skill frontmatter and relative reference links. Discovery depends on the host.
- [Skills directory](https://skills.sh/). Use it to find teaching skills, then
  assess their guidance before using them.

These are references, not setup instructions. The learner decides whether to
request a solution and whether to record progress.
